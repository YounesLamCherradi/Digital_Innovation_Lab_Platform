## About the Project

The **Digital Innovation Lab (DIL) Web Platform** is a comprehensive institutional solution developed to strengthen the digital presence, operational efficiency, and innovation ecosystem of Al Akhawayn University.

More than a conventional university website, the platform provides a centralized environment where students, faculty members, researchers, administrators, and external partners can discover opportunities, explore initiatives, engage with the Lab, and contribute to its activities.

The project reflects AUI's commitment to academic excellence, digital innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and meaningful connections between education, research, and industry.

By bringing institutional content, applications, communication, and administrative workflows together, the platform helps the Digital Innovation Lab operate through a coherent and user-friendly digital experience.

## Project Vision

**One digital platform connecting innovation, talent, research, and opportunity.**

The platform aims to:

- Strengthen the visibility of research and innovation activities at AUI.
- Connect students with academic and professional opportunities.
- Facilitate collaboration between faculty, researchers, and industry partners.
- Simplify institutional administrative workflows.
- Improve communication between the Lab and its community.
- Provide a scalable foundation for future digital initiatives.

## Platform Features

### 1. Institutional Website

A modern digital presence representing the Digital Innovation Lab and its activities.

- Interactive homepage and institutional presentation
- Information about the Lab's mission and objectives
- Responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Accessible navigation and organized information
- Visual storytelling through images, multimedia, and interactive elements
- Consistent branding aligned with the university's identity

### 2. Research Projects & Innovation Showcase

A dedicated environment for presenting research initiatives, innovative projects, and collaborative achievements.

- Project descriptions and objectives
- Research areas and project categories
- Project development status
- Multimedia project presentations
- Links to approved external resources
- Centralized visibility for student and faculty initiatives

**Institutional value:** Promotes academic achievements, improves project discoverability, and highlights the innovative work taking place within the university.

### 3. Opportunities & Recruitment Management

A centralized environment for publishing and managing opportunities offered through the Digital Innovation Lab.

- Publication of internships, research opportunities, and other available positions
- Opportunity descriptions and eligibility criteria
- Application deadlines and academic requirements
- Opportunity-specific application forms
- Search and exploration of available positions
- Clear application eligibility indicators

Opportunities can be designated as:

- **Open to Everyone:** Available to eligible applicants from inside or outside AUI.
- **AUI Students Only:** Applications restricted to university email addresses.

**Institutional value:** Makes opportunities easier to discover and supports an organized recruitment experience.

### 4. Dynamic Application Forms

A flexible application system designed to accommodate different types of opportunities.

Administrators can customize application requirements without modifying the platform's code.

Supported fields include:

- Full name and email address
- International phone number
- Curriculum vitae (PDF)
- Cover letter
- GitHub profile
- Portfolio URL
- Grade Point Average (GPA)
- Academic transcripts (PDF)
- Additional customized questions

Each opportunity can have its own application form, with configurable field visibility, mandatory or optional requirements, and question ordering.

**Institutional value:** Reduces unnecessary application requirements and ensures that each opportunity collects information relevant to its selection process.

### 5. Application Review & Applicant Management

A centralized administrative environment for managing submitted applications.

- Application submission and tracking
- Applicant profiles and submitted information
- Application review and status updates
- Acceptance and rejection workflows
- Internal administrative notes
- Individual and group communications
- Application history and document review

**Institutional value:** Improves the organization, consistency, and efficiency of application-processing activities.

### 6. News, Announcements & Publications

A dedicated area for sharing developments from the Digital Innovation Lab.

- Institutional news and announcements
- Research and innovation highlights
- Articles and publications
- Featured content
- Publication management
- Scheduled content preparation

**Institutional value:** Keeps the AUI community informed and reinforces the Lab's visibility and engagement.

### 7. Laboratory Team Directory

An organized presentation of the individuals contributing to the Digital Innovation Lab.

- Team member profiles
- Academic and professional roles
- Research interests and expertise
- Board members, research team, and Lab staff
- Professional profile links
- Configurable display order

**Institutional value:** Highlights the expertise behind the Lab and makes it easier to identify potential collaborators.

### 8. Strategic Partners & Collaboration

A dedicated space for recognizing institutional and industry partnerships.

- Partner profiles and institutional identities
- Collaboration highlights
- Partner categories
- Links to relevant organizations
- Visibility management

**Institutional value:** Strengthens connections between the university, research organizations, and the professional ecosystem.

### 9. Events & Institutional Activities

An organized presentation of the Lab's events, achievements, and community activities.

- Event information and descriptions
- Past-event galleries
- Institutional milestones
- Multimedia highlights
- Event-related content management

**Institutional value:** Preserves the Lab's institutional history and highlights its engagement with the academic community.

### 10. Communication & Notifications

Communication capabilities integrated into key platform workflows.

- Application receipt confirmations
- Administrative notifications
- Applicant acceptance and rejection messages
- Individual applicant emails
- Group communications
- Customizable notification templates
- Email previews and controlled test messaging

**Institutional value:** Supports timely, professional, and consistent communication with applicants and other stakeholders.

### 11. Centralized Administration Dashboard

An administrative workspace designed to support the day-to-day management of the Digital Innovation Lab.

Administrative capabilities include:

- Website content management
- Project and opportunity management
- Application review and communication
- Team member management
- Partner management
- Event content administration
- Notification template customization
- Dashboard user management

The platform supports differentiated administrative responsibilities through designated user roles.

**Institutional value:** Reduces dependence on developers for routine content updates and gives authorized Lab personnel greater operational autonomy.

- Professional and consistent visual identity
- Clear information hierarchy

- Intuitive navigation
- Smooth and purposeful interactions

- Consistent administrative interfaces

The objective is to provide a digital experience that reflects the university's innovative character while remaining practical for everyday institutional use.
